04 / Good to know

Quick answers

Appointments?

Absolutely! Booking ahead is recommended for larger pieces and custom tattoo projects. Walk-ins are always welcome!

What to bring?

Photo ID, a water bottle, snacks, and comfortable clothing. Get a good night's sleep and stay hydrated before your appointment!

How much?

Shop minimum: $120. Your tattoo price will be confirmed before we begin.

Payment?

We accept cash, debit, and all major credit cards.

05 / About us

About us

Love More · Est. 2020One More · Est. 2025

One More is the walk-in studio of Love More Tattoo, a Vancouver studio built on custom work since 2020.

After five years of by-appointment projects, we opened One More in Gastown in 2025 for everyone who doesn't want to wait. Same standards, same hygiene, same love for the craft, just with the door wide open.

Walk in for flash or a small piece, or book ahead for something bigger. Resident artists, guest artists and a dedicated piercer, all under one roof at 306 Water St.
